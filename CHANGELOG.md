## 1.7.3 (2026-10-30)

First release.

- **Does it hold up?** Pick who you're checking (a seller, a recruiter or job, a landlord, a website or shop, or yourself), give one thing you know, and get a verdict: Holds up, Doesn't add up or Red flags, with numbered reasons.
- **Proof.** Every find is saved as an exhibit with a page snapshot, a Wayback Machine copy, the time in UTC and a SHA-256 hash.
- **Follow the lead.** Explained pivots, 1 to 3 steps deep, with branches you can cut.
- **Timeline.** Every date found, on one line, with fresh clusters called out.
- **Check yourself.** Opt-out links for people-search sites, old accounts and known breaches.
- **Watch** for yourself, your own domains and companies, with an end date.
- PDF report with exhibits, hashes and the case log.
- Local AI through Ollama, or no AI at all.
- 3,412 public sources at launch.
- The Windows build: one portable `.exe`.
